# Walkthrough, Inspection

### Walkthrough
- A technique for reviewing development artifacts and finding defects `while they are being created`.
- Typically performed at the author's request, targeting intermediate artifacts.
- Inspection of subsequent work may be omitted.
- A less formal peer review method compared to inspection.

<br>

### Inspection
- The most formal review technique, inspected by experts other than the author.
- Based on documented procedures.
- Verifies whether software specifications are met.
- Requires more than double the personnel until coding begins, but due to subsequent reduction in personnel, it ultimately reduces quality costs and shortens development time.
- Management is excluded from inspection members.
