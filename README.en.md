## estudy.

This estudy repository is a collection of records I wrote while researching and studying the discipline of software.
From simple theoretical notes to deep dives and research, personal thoughts and appeals, and values and experiences I consider important are also contained here.
Lately, I've been conceiving the concept of a "developer's fast lane," and I'm asking myself if recording (記錄) can break the commandments of scale and entry.

Of course, if the act of recording itself were merely a means to show off to someone or satisfy intellectual vanity, I wouldn't have created this repository.
However, as trees are planted to some extent, and I see the forest gradually taking shape, it feels as if my hidden desires are being revealed.
There are times I want to write something I'm proud of, and I also write because I find intrinsic value in the act of recording itself.

Whether the motivation is internal or external, one thing is certain: the data of these records themselves become eternal the moment they are committed here, and
regardless of whether the content, structure, or format differs, everything in this repository is precious and valuable data.
The introduction has become long. In any case, this repository is simply a collection of records with various values as a software developer.
Some content might not be helpful to you, but I hope some might become a turning point.

"I sincerely welcome and thank you for visiting estudy, my forest of knowledge."
